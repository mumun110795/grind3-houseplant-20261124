## About this entry

The line keeps the out-of-office form completely straight — "I will be away until…", "for urgent matters…" — and lets the plant's own logic do the damage. A desk plant is never really away: it cannot leave, so its absence is just the stretch of days until somebody bothers to rotate it. And its only genuinely urgent matter is water, plus being turned so its good side faces the window. The comedy is office ritual read through photosynthesis-era priorities: a return date measured in rotations, and an escalation path that ends in a watering can.
